Privacy information

The public gallery displays images and does not require a user account. Gallery searches and style selections are processed in your browser. If you choose to purchase a digital edition, you leave this site for Stripe Checkout. Stripe collects and processes the payment information needed for the transaction under its own privacy notice.

To fulfill a purchase, the gallery's server receives a Stripe Checkout session identifier, checks that the payment succeeded and uses a private storage provider to issue a short-lived download link. Secret payment and storage keys are held on the server, not in the public browser code. Order records and provider logs may be retained as required for transaction support, accounting and fraud prevention.

The gallery uses its hosting provider and private object storage. No advertising trackers or marketing mailing list are included in this project. A full operator contact, data-retention periods and any required contractual and cross-border transfer disclosures should be completed based on the final hosting and merchant configuration before sales go live.

For privacy or purchase questions, use the contact options at freddybremseth.com.
