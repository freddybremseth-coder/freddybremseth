Privacy information

The public gallery does not require a user account. Gallery searches and style selections are processed in your browser. If you purchase an edition, Stripe Checkout processes the payment and, for a physical print, collects the delivery details required to complete the order.

For digital purchases, the gallery server receives a Stripe Checkout session identifier, verifies payment and uses private object storage to issue a short-lived download link. For physical print orders, the server sends the recipient name, delivery address, selected product and a temporary private image URL to Prodigi so the print can be manufactured and shipped. Payment card data is handled by Stripe rather than stored by the gallery.

Secret payment, storage and print-provider keys are held server-side and are not included in public browser code. Transaction and provider records may be retained as required for fulfilment, support, accounting, fraud prevention and legal obligations. Prodigi may retain production image assets for a limited period as part of its fulfilment process.

The gallery uses hosting and private object-storage providers. No advertising trackers or marketing mailing list are included in this project. A full operator contact, retention schedule and any required contractual or international-transfer disclosures must be completed based on the final merchant configuration before physical sales are switched live.

For privacy or purchase questions, use the contact options at freddybremseth.com.
