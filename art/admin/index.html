GALLERY CONTROL ROOM

Add art. Set the story.

Upload image files or one ZIP archive. You choose the collection and artistic style yourself—there is no automatic AI category guessing. High-resolution masters are stored privately; only reduced WebP previews are published.

—catalogue works
01

Choose files

Add original JPG, PNG or WebP files, or a ZIP containing them. ZIPs are extracted in your browser; the archive itself is never published. For ZIPs with several sizes of the same artwork, a single higher-resolution source is chosen.

Up to 30 works per batch · 250 MB per ZIP · 50 MB per image. For larger archives, split the ZIP or select individual files.

02

Choose the category

These values are always your decision. The selected values are applied to the current batch, and you can change them for individual images below.

Digital sales are not automatically activated. Uploaded masters stay private until purchase and delivery are separately verified.

03

Review each artwork

Titles, collections and styles can be corrected individually. Duplicate filenames are grouped; if a filename does not identify the correct existing work, choose it manually.

04

Your gallery catalogue

Existing works are listed below. Choose a work to review or adjust its manually selected category and style.
