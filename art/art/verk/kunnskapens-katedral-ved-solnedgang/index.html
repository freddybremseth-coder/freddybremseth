THE ARTIST'S APPROACH

Look closer.
Feel deeper.

My art begins with an emotion, a contradiction, or a question that refuses an easy answer. Every composition is developed as a story: a door can be a choice; a crack can be a place where light enters.

These are digitally created works developed with AI-assisted techniques under my creative direction, drawing on painterly methods and visual traditions to form new compositions — not copies of existing paintings.
